Decarbonising an already-green plant: Acciaieria Arvedi, Cremona

The first steelmaker with a certified net-zero claim. I set the claim aside, rebuilt the EAF plant's physical Scope 1 baseline from EU ETS data and costed a 2030 abatement package.
