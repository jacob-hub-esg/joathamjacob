Featured case study · 2026

Decarbonising an already-green plant: Acciaieria Arvedi, Cremona

Arvedi's Cremona EAF steelworks was the first steelmaker to hold a certified net-zero claim. I set the reported figure aside, rebuilt the physical Scope 1 baseline from EU ETS verified emissions, and costed a 2030 abatement package.

Models, tools and field work

MACC · 2026

Abatement cost analysis for a mid-market EAF steel plant

I modelled eight interventions for a 500,000 t/yr plant against a 150,000 tCO2e baseline at an 8% discount rate. Sensitivity on EU allowance prices shows the carbon price at which each capital-intensive measure turns viable.

~6,000 tCO2e/yr of negative-cost abatement found, for €2.7M capex
