Why it matters now

Every 2026 import has to be declared by September 2027

The data has to come from your suppliers' plants. Without it, you pay on the EU's default values, which carry a growing mark-up.

50 t

Importers bringing in 50 tonnes or more a year of iron and steel, aluminium, cement or fertilisers must be authorised CBAM declarants.

30 Sep 2027

Deadline for the first annual CBAM declaration, covering everything imported in 2026.

+10% → +30%

Mark-up on default values when supplier data is missing: 10% for 2026 imports, 20% for 2027 and 30% from 2028.

Regulation (EU) 2023/956 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2025/2083; default values and mark-ups in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2621.
