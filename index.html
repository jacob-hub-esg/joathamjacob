Featured case study · 2026

Decarbonising an already-green plant: Acciaieria Arvedi, Cremona

Arvedi's Cremona EAF steelworks was the first steelmaker to hold a certified net-zero claim. I set the reported figure aside, rebuilt the physical Scope 1 baseline from EU ETS verified emissions, and costed a 2030 abatement package.

Tools I've built, and field work

Decarbonization · 2026

MACC and project finance tool

Builds a marginal abatement cost curve with measures ordered cheapest first and bar width set by annual abatement. For each measure it calculates payback, NPV at two discount rates, IRR, LCOE and levelised cost of abatement, and updates live as the EU ETS price or measures change.

ReactViteSVG charts
Buildings · 2026

Building emissions benchmark & decarbonisation tool

Scope 1 and 2 emissions for buildings in 20 European countries and six building types, using country-specific IEA grid factors. It benchmarks carbon intensity against EU peers, checks CSRD readiness and projects a pathway to net zero by 2050.

GHG ProtocolESRS E1React
ESG reporting · 2026

CSRD readiness assessment

About 40 weighted questions across all ten ESRS topics. It scores readiness from 0 to 100 for each section and overall, flags the priority gaps with recommended actions, and shows the CSRD reporting timeline for each company category.

ESRSJavaScript
Buildings · 2026

CO2 benchmark calculator

Operational carbon for commercial buildings from type, floor area, energy source, age and occupancy. It returns tonnes of CO2e, kg CO2e/m² and energy use intensity by scope, rated against EU Taxonomy, EPBD and BREEAM thresholds.

GHG ProtocolEU TaxonomyReact

Energy performance monitoring pipeline

A Python pipeline that takes hourly meter data and builds a weather-normalised baseline by degree-day regression. It flags deviations from expected consumption and reports them in Power BI, in the form of ISO 50001 energy performance monitoring.
